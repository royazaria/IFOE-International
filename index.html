Our Story

Born from Brotherhood

The International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles (IFOE) is a proud branch rooted in the enduring legacy of the original Philippine Eagles movement, founded in Quezon City in 1979. Under the visionary leadership of Attorney Jun Salatandre, IFOE was established to carry that flame onto a truly international stage.

"We are Eagles by heritage, international by mission — and brothers by heart."

Though only a year and a half old, IFOE has already united clubs across regions, hosted its inaugural International Convention, and inducted leaders committed to Humanitarian Service and Deo Et Patria — God and Country.

1979
Original Philippine Eagles founded in Quezon City by 13 visionary leaders at Aberdeen Court, Quezon Avenue.
2025
IFOE Philippine Eagles established by Atty. Jun Salatandre. 1st International Convention held — clubs chartered, leadership inducted under one banner.
2026
2nd International Convention & 1st Anniversary — March 7–9, 2026. Celebrated with PBA legends and dignitaries.

Edward Joseph "E.J." Feihl is the tallest Filipino professional basketball player to have ever competed in the PBA — a towering 7'1" center of German-Filipino descent who became one of the most distinctive figures in Philippine basketball history. Drafted 2nd overall in 1995, Feihl played 13 seasons, won 3 PBA championship rings, and represented the Philippines in the 1998 Bangkok Asian Games, bringing home a bronze medal with the national team. The gentle giant who once dominated paint now stands tall within the Eagle brotherhood.

Nelson "The Bull" Asaytono is one of the most explosive and highest-scoring forwards in PBA history. Drafted 2nd overall by Purefoods in 1989, he powered through 17 dominant seasons — scoring 12,668 career points (5th all-time), winning 7 championships, earning 10 All-Star appearances and 3 Mythical First Team selections. In April 2025, he was rightfully enshrined in the PBA 50 Greatest Players list, one of Philippine basketball's most prestigious honors. The Bull's relentless drive mirrors the spirit of brotherhood and service that defines the Eagles.

Who We Are

Brotherhood Beyond Borders

The International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles is a socio-civic organization built on timeless values of service, brotherhood, and mutual care. Rooted in the original Philippine Eagles movement of 1979, IFOE carries this legacy forward on the world stage.

"Eagleism is fraternalism — that state of relationship characteristic of brothers."

From medical missions in remote communities to international chartering ceremonies, our Eagles carry the spirit of service wherever they soar — united not by geography, but by a shared calling to uplift lives and serve with honor.
