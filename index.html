Our Story

Born from Brotherhood

The International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles (IFOE) is a proud branch rooted in the enduring legacy of the original Philippine Eagles movement, founded in Quezon City in 1979. Under the visionary leadership of Attorney Jun Salatandre, IFOE was established to carry that flame onto a truly international stage — bringing the spirit of Service Through Strong Brotherhood to Filipinos and communities across the globe.

"We are Eagles by heritage, international by mission — and brothers by heart."

Though only a year and a half old, IFOE has already united clubs across regions, hosted its inaugural International Convention and 1st Anniversary, and inducted leaders devoted to the principles of Humanitarian Service and Deo Et Patria — God and Country.

Who We Are

Brotherhood Beyond Borders

The International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles is a socio-civic organization built on timeless values of service, brotherhood, and mutual care. Rooted in the original Philippine Eagles movement of 1979, IFOE carries this legacy forward on the world stage.

"Eagleism is fraternalism — that state of relationship characteristic of brothers."

From medical missions in remote communities to international chartering ceremonies, our Eagles carry the spirit of service wherever they soar — united not by geography, but by a shared calling to uplift lives and serve with honor.
