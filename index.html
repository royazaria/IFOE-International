Who We Are

Brotherhood Beyond Borders

The International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles is a socio-civic organization founded on the timeless principles of service, brotherhood, and mutual care. Born from the heart of the Philippines in 1979, we have grown into an international movement with chapters across the globe.

"Eagleism is fraternalism — that state of relationship characteristic of brothers."

From medical missions in remote barangays to international chartering ceremonies in the UAE, our Eagles carry the spirit of service wherever they soar. We are united not by geography, but by a shared calling: to uplift lives, strengthen communities, and serve with honor.

What We Do

Our Programs

From disaster relief to education, from medical missions to environmental conservation — the Eagles serve where they are needed most.

Flagship Program
🏥
Medical Missions
Free consultations, treatments and health education for remote and underserved communities across the Philippines and beyond.
Education
📚
Brigada Eskwela
School supplies and resources for underprivileged students nationwide.
Crisis Response
🆘
Disaster Relief
Immediate aid, supplies and rebuilding support for communities in crisis.
Environment
🌿
Green Eagles
Tree-planting drives, coastal cleanups, and environmental conservation projects.
International
🌐
Global Chartering
Expanding the Eagle brotherhood to OFW communities worldwide — UAE, and beyond.
