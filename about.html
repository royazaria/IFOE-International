Our Roots

Born from 46 Years of History

The story of the Philippine Eagles goes back to 1979 — when 13 visionary leaders gathered at Aberdeen Court on Quezon Avenue in Quezon City and founded the original Philippine Eagles fraternity. For nearly five decades, that flame burned across the islands, building a culture of brotherhood, service, and Filipino pride.

In 2025, Attorney Jun Salatandre — a proud Eagle who understood both that legacy and its unrealized global potential — made a bold decision: to bring the Philippine Eagles onto the world stage. He founded the International Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles (IFOE), registering it with the SEC as the first Philippine-born international fraternal socio-civic organization in the country's history.

"We are Eagles by heritage, international by mission — and brothers by heart."

Within months, clubs were chartered from Cebu to Mindanao. A year later, the 1st International Convention was held in Cebu City — with delegates from the United States, Canada, and across the Philippines raising their hands together in the Eagles salute. The brotherhood had taken flight.

1979
Original Philippine Eagles founded in Quezon City by 13 visionaries at Aberdeen Court, Quezon Avenue. A fraternity of service and brotherhood takes root.
2025
IFOE Philippine Eagles established by Atty. Jun Salatandre. SEC-registered as the first Philippine-born international fraternal socio-civic organization. 1st International Convention held in Cebu City — clubs chartered, leaders inducted.
2026
2nd International Convention & 1st Anniversary — March 7–9, 2026. PBA legends, international delegates, and Eagles from every region united. The organization soars.

Who We Are

A Brotherhood Built on Purpose

The IFOE Philippine Eagles is not a social club. It is a mission-driven brotherhood — men and women bound by the conviction that service to God and Country is the highest calling a person can answer.

Every Eagle who takes the oath commits to three things: Humanitarian Service to their community, Brotherhood that crosses borders and backgrounds, and Deo et Patria — God and Country above self.

Our members are professionals, lawyers, business owners, athletes, and community leaders. What unites them is not status — it is the shared belief that a strong brotherhood can change lives.

Understanding the Distinction

IFOE Philippine Eagles vs. the "Regular" Eagles

We are often asked: "Are you the same as the Fraternal Order of Eagles in America?" The answer is both yes and no — and the distinction matters deeply.

Fraternal Order of Eagles (FOE)
Founded 1898 · Seattle, USA · American Origin
🇺🇸
Origin
Founded in Seattle, Washington in 1898 by a group of theater owners. Rooted in American culture and history.
🏛️
Structure
Headquartered in the United States. International presence primarily through US-based aeries and Canadian chapters.
📋
Focus
Primarily focused on North American communities, social programs, and longstanding American civic traditions.
🌐
Philippines Presence
Limited formal presence in the Philippines. Not designed to serve the specific needs of Filipino communities at home and abroad.
📅
Philippine Roots
No direct historical connection to the 1979 Philippine Eagles movement founded in Quezon City.
IFOE Philippine Eagles
Founded 2025 · Quezon City roots since 1979 · Philippine-Born
🇵🇭
Origin
Born in the Philippines — rooted in a proud 46-year local legacy dating back to 1979 Quezon City. A Filipino brotherhood, for Filipinos, by Filipinos.
🌏
Structure
Philippine-headquartered with an international mandate. Clubs in multiple Philippine regions with an open door for Filipino diaspora worldwide.
❤️
Focus
Deeply embedded in Filipino communities — feeding programs, medical missions, diplomatic courtesy calls, and grassroots humanitarian service.
🦅
Philippines Presence
Multiple active clubs across Cebu, the Visayas, and Mindanao — with more regions being chartered every year. This is home.
📜
Philippine Roots
Direct heir to the 1979 Philippine Eagles movement. SEC-registered as the first Philippine-born international fraternal socio-civic organization in history.

We respect and honor the legacy of the original Fraternal Order of Eagles. But the IFOE Philippine Eagles is something distinct: a Philippine-born, internationally-minded brotherhood — built not as a branch of something foreign, but as a proud Filipino institution that serves its own people while standing on the world stage.

Eagles in the Community

Brotherhood in action

Every outreach mission begins the same way: Eagles show up — in their black uniforms, bearing food, medicines, or simply their presence — into communities that rarely see organized help. They don't make speeches. They just work.

From Cebu's urban barangays to communities in Mindanao, the Eagles' outreach programs have touched hundreds of families. The work is not glamorous. It is carried out in heat, in tight alleys, with handshakes and smiles — and it is the truest expression of what being an Eagle means.

The Proof

What One Year Looks Like

Skeptics said it couldn't be done in a year. That building a truly international fraternal organization from scratch — with chapters, ceremonies, international delegates, and a registered identity — takes a decade.

The IFOE Philippine Eagles did it in twelve months.

At the 1st International Convention in January 2025, Eagles from across the Philippines and from abroad gathered in Cebu City. Officers were inducted in solemn ceremony. International delegates flew in. The oath was taken. The eagle had landed.

"In one year, we built what others take ten to build — because when Brothers move together, they move fast."

By March 2026, the 2nd International Convention & 1st Anniversary drew even more — including two of the Philippines' most legendary PBA players, standing side by side with Eagles in the brotherhood.
